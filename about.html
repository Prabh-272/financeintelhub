The Origin

This Is Not A Finance Blog
Run By Finance People.

FinanceIntelHub was not built in a boardroom or a business school. It was built by someone who was sitting with $50,000 in debt, no income, and no clear path forward.

Someone who spent 20 years working as a long-haul truck driver — building a life with their hands, their time, and their discipline — and then had to start completely over.

The finance content that exists online is mostly written by people who have never actually been broke. Theoretical strategies from people filming from offices and studios, selling courses for $997 to people who cannot afford $97.

We built this because that content failed us when we needed it most.

FinanceIntelHub is what we needed then. Written by someone who lived it. Priced for someone still in it.

Our Values

What We Believe

Real Over Polished

We would rather give you an honest uncomfortable truth than a comfortable lie dressed up in motivational language.

Accessible Over Expensive

Financial education should not cost more than you can afford. Our guides are priced for people who are still in the struggle — not people who have already escaped it.

Action Over Theory

Every guide we publish has to pass one test: can an ordinary person with no background act on this today? If the answer is no, we rewrite it.
