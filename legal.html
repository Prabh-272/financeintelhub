Section 06

Cookie Policy

6.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function correctly, remember user preferences, and provide site operators with analytics data. Some cookies are set by the website you are visiting (first-party cookies), while others are set by third-party services that the site uses.

6.2 Cookies We Use

Functional / Essential Cookies

  • cookieConsent — a first-party value stored in your browser's localStorage that records whether you have accepted our cookie notice. This prevents the consent banner from reappearing on every visit. It persists until you clear your browser data.

Analytics Cookies — Google Analytics (Google LLC)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website. The following cookies may be set by Google Analytics:

  • _ga — a persistent cookie used to distinguish unique users by assigning a randomly generated identifier. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_[ID] — used by Google Analytics 4 to persist session state. Expires after 2 years.
  • _gid — used to distinguish users. Expires after 24 hours.
  • _gat — used to throttle the rate of Google Analytics requests. Expires after 1 minute.

Google Analytics collects this information anonymously. We have enabled IP anonymisation, which means IP addresses are truncated before being stored. The data collected may include pages visited, session duration, approximate geographic location (country/region), device type, browser, and referring source. Google may transfer this data to servers in the United States. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics opt-out options.

Marketing / Tracking Cookies — Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.)

If you subscribe to our email newsletter, Mailchimp may place tracking technologies in our emails to record whether you opened an email and whether you clicked any links within it. This helps us understand the effectiveness of our email campaigns and improve our content. Mailchimp may also use cookies when you visit pages linked from our emails. See Mailchimp's Privacy Policy for full details.

6.3 How to Control and Disable Cookies

You have several options to control how cookies are used:

Browser settings: All modern browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences. You can block all cookies, accept only certain categories, or receive alerts before a cookie is placed. Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Google Analytics opt-out: You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your website visits by installing the free Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Email tracking opt-out: To prevent Mailchimp email-level tracking, you can disable loading of remote images in your email client settings, or unsubscribe from our newsletter entirely.

EU users who object to the placement of analytics cookies may opt out at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. We make reasonable efforts to comply with applicable cookie consent requirements across all jurisdictions we serve.

6.4 Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies or opt out of analytics tracking, our website will continue to function normally for the most part. However, you should be aware of the following potential impacts:

  • Your cookie consent preference will not be saved, meaning the consent banner may reappear each time you visit our site.
  • We will have less data available to understand how visitors use our site, reducing our ability to improve the user experience over time.
  • Some third-party features or embedded content linked to from our site may not function correctly.

6.5 Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent notice will appear at the bottom of the page. By clicking "Accept," you consent to our use of analytics and functional cookies as described in this policy. Your consent preference is stored in your browser's localStorage. You may withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage and cookies, and revisiting our site.

This section is specifically for users in the European Union (EU) and United Kingdom (UK), whose personal data is protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the UK GDPR respectively. Canadian users are protected under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). California residents are protected under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

We respect your data protection rights and are committed to upholding them. Below is a full explanation of your rights and how to exercise them.

7.1 Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, along with information about how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how long it is retained. 7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")

You have the right to request that we permanently delete your personal data. This right applies in the following circumstances:

  • The data is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was originally collected or processed;
  • You withdraw your consent and there is no other lawful basis for continued processing;
  • You object to the processing under Article 21 GDPR and there are no overriding legitimate grounds;
  • The data has been unlawfully processed;
  • Deletion is required to comply with a legal obligation under EU or UK law.

7.4 Right to Restrict Processing

You have the right to request that we pause or restrict the processing of your personal data in certain defined circumstances:

  • While we verify the accuracy of data you have contested;
  • Where the processing is unlawful but you prefer restriction over erasure;
  • Where we no longer need the data but you require it for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims;
  • Where you have objected to processing and we are assessing whether our legitimate grounds override yours.

7.5 Right to Data Portability

Where we process your personal data based on your consent or on the performance of a contract, and that processing is carried out by automated means, you have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (such as CSV or JSON). You also have the right to request that we transmit that data directly to another data controller, where technically feasible.

7.6 Right to Object

You have the right to object at any time to our processing of your personal data where that processing is based on our legitimate interests or on the performance of a task in the public interest. If you object, we must stop processing your data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights, and freedoms — or unless the processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

You have an absolute right to object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes at any time, with no exceptions. If you exercise this right, we will immediately and permanently cease using your data for direct marketing, including any profiling carried out for marketing purposes.

7.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

You have the right not to be subject to a decision made solely on the basis of automated processing — including profiling — where that decision produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you. FinanceIntelHub does not currently engage in any automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Where we rely on your consent as the lawful basis for processing your personal data (for example, for newsletter subscriptions and email marketing communications), you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.
